GOA FLOW · QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Everything worth
knowing before you go.

Here are the questions guests ask most often before booking. If you need help with a specific date or room, message us directly.

What is included in the retreat price?

Accommodation, three vegetarian meals a day, practices, two excursions and programme transfers are included. Flights, insurance, visa and personal expenses are separate.

How do I choose dates and book a place?

Choose a suitable date on the home page and message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, answer room questions and explain the next step. A non-refundable $150 deposit is required and is included in the total price.

Is the retreat suitable if I am new to yoga?

Yes. The practices are designed for different levels of experience. Tell us about any health considerations or limitations in advance so we can guide you carefully.

What accommodation options are available?

There are triple, twin, double, single and single LUX rooms. Current prices are listed in Dates & prices; availability depends on the selected retreat dates.

How is food organised?

The Goa Flow team prepares three full vegetarian meals a day, with fresh vegetables, fruit, greens and Indian dishes. Tell us about dietary needs before the retreat.

How long is the retreat?

Each standard arrival is 12 days and 11 nights, with practices, ocean time, free time, group meetings, excursions and room to rest.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Many guests arrive independently. During the first days the group gets to know one another, while the team helps everyone feel comfortable and supported.
