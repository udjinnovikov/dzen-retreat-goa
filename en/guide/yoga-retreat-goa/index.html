GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

How to choose a
yoga retreat in Goa.

There is no universal retreat programme. The important thing is that the pace, practices, people and conditions match why you want to travel.

LOOK BEYOND THE PHOTOS

Ask five simple questions.

Who leads the practices? What does an ordinary day look like? What is included? Where will you stay? Is there unstructured time to rest?

What to look for

  • Programme: a clear balance of practices, sea time, excursions and free time.
  • Teachers: real profiles, experience and an opportunity to ask questions before booking.
  • Conditions: room photos, food format, transfers, accommodation rules and transparent pricing.
  • Group: its size, atmosphere and pace.
  • Flexibility: the freedom to skip an activity when rest is what you need.

Who Goa Flow is for

Goa Flow is a 12-day Ashwem retreat for people who want a gentle reset: yoga, breathwork, vegetarian food, ocean time, excursions and a warm group. Beginners are welcome.

Before booking

See the team, participant stories, dates and accommodation options, then message us. We will explain the format honestly and help you decide whether the retreat fits.
