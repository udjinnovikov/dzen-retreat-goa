A balanced 12-day programme

Mornings begin with body and breath. The programme includes yoga, sound practices, hammock yoga, creative and women’s practices, sea time, shared meals and unhurried evenings. Every practice is optional.

Who it is for

  • Beginners and experienced practitioners.
  • Solo travellers who would like to arrive with a group.
  • People who want wellbeing without a rigid schedule.
  • Guests looking for a warm retreat in India.

Stay close to the ocean

You will stay in a green hotel with a pool, bright rooms and the beach a few minutes away. Twin, double and single room options are available.
