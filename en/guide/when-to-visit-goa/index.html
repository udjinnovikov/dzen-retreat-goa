GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

When to visit
Goa.

For many travellers, November to March offers the most comfortable beach-weather rhythm. The right month still depends on whether you want lively events, a quieter stay, greener landscapes or a more flexible budget.

Choose by your pace

Plan around your energy, budget and preferred group atmosphere, not just a weather forecast. During the busier weeks, book key accommodation and airport transfers earlier than usual.

How the seasons feel

  • November to February: a popular period for beach days, outdoor events and a full seasonal programme.
  • March and April: warmer days and a slightly slower rhythm; plan outdoor activities around the heat.
  • Monsoon months: greener landscapes and a different Goa experience, with more rain and a changed beach and events calendar.

Before you choose dates

Check the current forecast, transport options and the opening dates of the places you want to visit. December and early January are popular, so availability and prices can change quickly. If your dates are flexible, compare two nearby weeks.

For a calmer group rhythm, ask about the specific retreat dates rather than assuming every week feels the same. See how to check the current events programme before booking.
