GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

When to visit
Goa.

For many travellers, November to March offers the most comfortable beach-weather rhythm. The right month still depends on whether you want livelier events, a quieter stay or greener monsoon landscapes.

Choose by your pace.

Plan around your energy, budget and preferred group atmosphere, not just a weather forecast. Book key accommodation and airport transfers in advance during the busier months.

What to consider

  • Weather and sea conditions change through the year.
  • December and early January are popular, so rooms and transport book sooner.
  • For a calmer group rhythm, ask about the specific retreat dates rather than assuming every week feels the same.
