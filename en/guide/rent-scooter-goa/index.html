GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Renting a scooter
in Goa.

A scooter gives you freedom, but calm preparation matters more than speed in an unfamiliar place. Use this checklist to ask the right questions before you ride.

Before you pay

Ask which documents the rental company accepts, what is included, the deposit amount, return conditions and how a breakdown is handled. Check the requirements of your driving licence, permit and travel insurance before travelling; they depend on your citizenship and vehicle category.

Inspect the vehicle

Photograph the scooter from every side before leaving. Test brakes, lights, indicators, tyres, mirrors and fuel level. Ask for a properly fitting helmet. Goa’s transport and police guidance makes helmet use a legal and practical safety requirement for two-wheelers.

Your first ride

Traffic in India can feel very different. Make the first ride in daylight, on a short familiar route and without a deadline. Do not use a phone while riding, never ride after alcohol, and choose a taxi when you are tired or unsure.

A simple rule

If the documents, vehicle condition or rental terms feel unclear, walk away and choose another option. A good rental should make the process transparent, not rushed.
