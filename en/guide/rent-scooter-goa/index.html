GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Renting a scooter
in Goa.

A scooter gives you freedom, but calm preparation matters more than speed in an unfamiliar place. Use this checklist to ask the right questions before you ride.

Before you pay

Ask which documents the rental company accepts, what is included, the deposit amount, return conditions and how a breakdown is handled. Check the requirements of your driving licence, permit and travel insurance before travelling; they depend on your citizenship and vehicle category.

Compare the total terms

Ask whether the price is per calendar day, how late returns are handled and whether fuel, a second helmet or roadside help costs extra. Read the agreement and keep the owner’s contact details. Do not leave your original passport as a deposit.

Inspect the vehicle

Photograph the scooter from every side before leaving. Test brakes, lights, indicators, tyres, mirrors, horn and fuel level. Ask for a properly fitting helmet. If anything feels unsafe or the paperwork is unclear, choose another rental.

Your first ride

Make the first ride in daylight, on a short familiar route and without a deadline. Traffic can feel very different: keep a wide buffer, expect vehicles to merge and never use a phone while riding. Do not ride after alcohol; choose a taxi when you are tired or unsure.

Return it clearly

Agree the return point and time in writing. Take a final set of photos, settle the fuel level and ask for confirmation that the deposit has been returned. A transparent rental should never feel rushed.

For official requirements, check current Goa transport guidance and your own country’s licence rules.
