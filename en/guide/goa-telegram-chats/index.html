Search more precisely

Use an area plus a topic: “Arambol accommodation”, “Mandrem scooter”, or “Goa events”. Join only a few groups first; it is easier to compare useful advice without an overwhelming feed.

Stay careful with bookings

  • Confirm the exact dates, location, final price and cancellation terms.
  • Do not transfer money to a stranger based on chat messages alone.
  • Check that advice is recent: seasons, prices and local rules change.
