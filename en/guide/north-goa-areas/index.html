GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Where to stay
in North Goa.

North Goa has very different rhythms within short distances. Choose an area by how you want your days to feel, rather than by a single “best” recommendation.

Ashwem

For a quieter beach rhythm, more open space and an easy base for a restorative stay. Goa Flow is based here.

Mandrem and Arambol

Mandrem often suits travellers looking for a relaxed, village-like pace. Arambol is more social and active, with a wider range of gatherings, music and people passing through.

Before you decide

  • Check the distance to the beach, food and your planned activities.
  • Ask about road access and airport transfer times.
  • Choose the atmosphere you want in the evening as well as during the day.
