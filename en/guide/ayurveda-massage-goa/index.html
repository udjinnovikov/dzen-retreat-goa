GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Ayurveda and massage
in Goa.

In Goa, “Ayurveda” can mean very different things: a relaxing spa massage, a consultation, or a structured wellness programme. It helps to know which experience you are actually looking for before booking.

A USEFUL PRINCIPLE

Relaxation is not medical treatment.

Wellness treatments do not replace diagnosis or medical care. If you have a chronic condition, are pregnant, have allergies or acute pain, speak to a qualified clinician first and share relevant contraindications with the practitioner.

Choose the right format

A spa massage can be a pleasant way to unwind after travel. If you are seeking an Ayurvedic approach, look for a centre that offers an initial consultation, explains the goal of each treatment and discusses the oils or methods used. Be cautious with blanket health promises.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Who delivers the treatment and what is their experience or qualification?
  • What are the duration, total price, oils used and possible contraindications?
  • Is there a consultation, and how is hygiene handled?
  • Can the treatment be adapted or stopped if you feel unwell?
