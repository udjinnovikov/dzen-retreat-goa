03 / PROGRAMME

12 days with
room for you.

01—02Arrival and connection+

Airport pickup, transfer, check-in, rest after the journey and a first introduction to the group.

03—04Gentle immersion+

Practices, ocean time, meals, free time and an easy arrival into the rhythm of Goa.

05—08Life in Goa and discovery+

Walks, local places, day trips, excursions, markets, nature and sunsets.

09—12Integration and closing+

Practices, rest, evening gatherings, time for yourself, farewell and transfer.
